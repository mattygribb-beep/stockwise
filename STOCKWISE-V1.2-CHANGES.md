# Stockwise V1.2 — Purchasing Intelligence

V1.2 extends the fixed V1.1 buying engine into a purchase-outcome loop.

## Added
- Opportunity lifecycle statuses
- Mark-as-purchased workflow
- Completed outcome tracking with expected-vs-actual selling prices
- Monthly buying budget and capital-impact display
- Minimum margin and first-purchase-capital buying rules
- Selling-price source and UNVERIFIED manual-estimate indicator
- TEST BUY logic considers confidence/exposure as well as economics
- Portfolio capital, realised outcomes and purchase-history views
- Stock Memory repeat-EAN recognition

## Retained
- Correct Buying Bar / decision direction
- Settings-driven decision engine
- EAN/barcode capture
- Wholesale Opportunities workspace
- Supplier intelligence concepts
